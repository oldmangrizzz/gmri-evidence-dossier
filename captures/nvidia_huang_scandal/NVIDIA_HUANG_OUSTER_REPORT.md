

# NVIDIA / JENSEN HUANG — OUSTER-SCANDAL UPDATE (2026-11-23, post-Amazon/NVDA pass)

**This section covers breaking developments** in the NVIDIA/Jensen Huang scandal since the Part C of the Amazon/NVIDIA pass was written (it did not capture this story).

**Charter rule preserved:** all allegations here are allegations, carried with the company's/Musk's own responses. No personal-misconduct claim is asserted as fact. The story is breaking and is recorded at its captured tier.

## 1. The board's stated primary reason: "lying" (VERIFIED — WIRED, 2026-11-20)

The board's stated motivation was not the alleged affairs — it was the candor/integrity issue. From WIRED (1,282,423 B capture, VERIFIED quotes):

> "It was 'multiple issues' related to Huang's 'lack of candor' and 'lying' that ultimately moved the board of directors."
> 'While Huang admitted to having an affair with Alice Huang on November 8, the board couldn't determine in the moment whether she was an intelligence operative for China... In the following days, once allegations of other affairs surfaced, including one reportedly with a direct report, "what could be determined was [Huang's] lying, and that spanned a year, from October 2025 through recent events," a source close to the board told WIRED.'
> "It was a matter of integrity and trust," this person said.

## 2. Huang's own admission + willingness to return (VERIFIED — ABC News capture)

From ABC News (215,951 B capture, VERIFIED):
- Huang's on-record statement: "I did not live up to the standards expected of everyone at Nvidia, and I have made serious mistakes in my personal life."
- Further: "If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest..., I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO."
- The board rejected the offer — Harvey Jones in the earnings call: "the board 'unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.'"

## 3. The alleged Chinese intelligence-asset link

**Allegation (attributed, not adjudicated):** that Alice Huang (no relation to Jensen) was a "Chinese intelligence asset" tied to Megaspeed (Singapore data-center company with which NVIDIA had "an unusually robust business relationship"). China's Foreign Ministry: "The claim regarding a so-called Chinese spy is completely groundless and pure slander." — the Chinese rebuttal is carried.
- Reuters capture (544,327 B): Huang described relations as typical for customer interactions to leaders such as General Counsel Teter (captured); Huang urged legal department to weigh action against the New York Times over its reporting (sources say).
- **Megaspeed export-control probe:** DOJ/Commerce joint investigation began 2024; Megaspeed added to NVIDIA's "no sell" list June 2025; Commerce requested records May 2025.

## 4. House Foreign Affairs + Intelligence Committees: Huang + Teter summoned

**Both set for closed-door transcribed interviews (Dec 1 Huang, Dec 2 Teter).** Chairmen Brian Mast (FA), John Moolenaar (CCP), Rick Crawford (Intel): "The American people need answers on Mr. Huang and Nvidia's role in allowing dangerous PRC actors to access our most advanced chips, as well as the role of a potential Chinese intelligence asset in this scandal."
