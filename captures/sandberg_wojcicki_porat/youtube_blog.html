      YouTube partners with KidsRights for Children's Peace Prize

      Nov 19, 2026

      At the award ceremony at the Peace Palace in The Hague, 17-year-old Colombian climate activist Francisco Javier Vera Manzanares received the 2026 International Children’s Peace Prize by the KidsRights Foundation, the world’s most important youth prize. YouTube was proud to stand alongside KidsRights as a Supporting Partner, celebrating Francisco and finalists Issaiah Guzman Arancibia and Sufaira (surname withheld for personal safety) for their courage in standing up for children’s rights.

      Speaking at the ceremony, Katie Kurtz, YouTube’s Global Head of Youth and Learning, shared how honored YouTube is to support KidsRights, and to provide young leaders with tools to learn, collaborate, and advocate. "YouTube is proud to be both your library and your megaphone, today and for all the chapters of your story still to come," Kurtz said.

      We’re honored to support KidsRights in amplifying the extraordinary work of young changemakers worldwide.

      Steve Kornacki on the success of his U.S. midterms YouTube livestream

      By Rene Ritchie, YouTube Creator Liaison

      Nov 10, 2026

      Election nights are fast moving, high stakes, and filled with data. When it came to breaking down the 2026 U.S. midterms, Steve Kornacki’s work on the Big Board was a staple of election night coverage. On YouTube, Kornacki and NBC News brought people closer to the action with "Kornacki Cam," an unfiltered, 10+ hour livestream giving viewers a front-row seat to realtime results.

      This behind-the-scenes coverage was the biggest election-related livestream on YouTube on November 3, 2026, peaking at over 350,000 concurrent viewers at 10:19 p.m. ET. We caught up with Steve (after he had some rest) for a look at what it takes to pull off Kornacki Cam.

      Kornacki was quick to point out that he and his team had a lot of practice and preparation for this night going all the way back to primaries over the winter. The most fun part, he says, is watching the community take shape. Being able to interact with viewers brings a different dynamic to his coverage, whether it’s a local primary or a national election.

      Watch the full Creator Insider interview with Steve Kornacki to learn more.

      What Europe’s creative entrepreneurs need to thrive

      Nov 09, 2026

      Across Europe, creators aren’t just posting videos — they are building the media businesses of the future. They hire camera crews, manage studios, and green light their own shows.

      Together, this creative community contributed over €8.4 billion to the EU’s GDP and supported more than 220,000 jobs in 2025. But too often, creators are left out from the decisions that impact the future of their businesses.

      When we surveyed 20,000 creators across all 27 EU member states as part of a landmark creator consultation, they spoke with one voice.

      Creators aren't asking for special privileges — they simply want policymakers to ensure a fair, level playing field for media businesses big and small.

      Watch their stories below to see what it takes to build a modern creative business, and how Europe can help them thrive.

      How viewers followed the U.S. midterm elections on YouTube

      Nov 06, 2026

      Throughout the U.S. midterm elections, people turned to YouTube to engage with candidates, unpack key issues, and follow pivotal races. On Election Day alone, over 35 million U.S. viewers watched election-related content on the platform. Globally, over 40 million viewers watched livestreams related to the U.S. midterm elections, with viewership peaking at over 3 million concurrent viewers at 10:19 p.m. ET.

      YouTube connected viewers directly to live coverage from national networks, local broadcasters, and independent news creators.
