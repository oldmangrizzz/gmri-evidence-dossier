Join our new community platform!

Lean In Connect is the new hub for Lean In's global community of career-minded women. Here, you can join or start a peer mentorship Circle or Network, meet women in your area or field, learning about upcoming events, and upskill yourself on everything from AI to salary negotiation. You'll also be among the first to hear about new research or product launches from Lean In. We'd love to see you there.

Subscribe to our newsletter!

The Lead is Lean In's weekly newsletter for people who are ready to take their careers to the next level and make change in their workplaces. You'll get Lean In’s original research, op-eds and Q&As from prominent voices on women and work, advice columns with reader-submitted questions, and community members sharing their workplace advice, mistakes, and salaries.
