## 3. THE LEGAL FRAMEWORK — PER-ROUTE EVALUATION

### 3.1 FCC CVAA (47 U.S.C. § 255 / § 617 [formerly known as "Section 716"], 47 CFR Part 14)

**Duty:** "Accessible to and usable by" individuals with disabilities under §255(b) if "readily achievable"; product-support communications under §14.20(d); complaint contact certification under §14.31.

**Ready-to-file:** FCC Request for Dispute Assistance (RDA) route. The Meta chat captures 14+ human-contact refusals during an account lockout — squarely a "product-support communications" accessibility failure.

**2026 NPRM** (FCC 26-76, adopted 2026-10-29, comment-stage) proposes to bring MFA and AI frontends into accessibility scope for the first time. This is **comment-stage**, not law. The 2026 Biennial Report ¶36 fn.139 confirms "AI-to-human messaging" is NOT "electronic messaging service" under § 153(56) — so a pure chatbot conversation is NOT ACS per current FCC interpretation.

**Practical route:** RDA against Meta. 14.36's 20-day answer duty. 47 CFR Part 14 captured in full from the eCFR versioner API.

### 3.2 TX DTPA (Tex. Bus. Com. Code ch. 17) and TDPSA (ch. 541)

**DTPA:** Treble damages + automatic attorney fees available. Enumerated acts at 17.46(b). Claim-shape: 17.46(b)(24) — concealing a known material fact to induce reliance (the Health page→Notice drift); 17.46(b)(5)/(7) — misrepresenting services have qualities they don't. §17.565: two-year SOL. §17.50(a): detrimental reliance and producing cause required.

**Critical:** TDPSA §541.156: *"This chapter may not be construed as providing a basis for, or being subject to, a private right of action for a violation of this chapter **or any other law**."* This forecloses any TDPSA-laundered claim AND forecloses DTPA claims framed as TDPSA violations. Only DTPA's enumerated items survive.

**TDPSA enforcement:** AG-exclusive (§541.151). H.B. 149 (eff. 2026-01-01) amended §541.104 to cover AI-system data processing — AG-side only.

**Claim-shape for the operator:** DTPA §17.50(b)(24) on Health-page → Privacy-Notice drift is the strongest private action. DTPA treble damages available. Two-year SOL from Nov 22 2026 = claimable conduct back to Nov 22 2024.

### 3.3 DOJ ADA Title III (42 U.S.C. §§ 12181, 12182) and Section 504 (29 U.S.C. § 794)

**Title III:** Requires a "place of public accommodation." **Magee v. Coca-Cola** (833 F.3d 530, 5th Cir. 2016; cert. denied No. 16-668, Oct 2 2017): the defendant must own/lease/operate the place; virtual-only entities do not qualify. The Fifth Circuit has **never decided the digital-service question** — the court expressly declined to address "facilities" under 28 CFR § 36.104. A Title III route in the Fifth Circuit is **high-risk, not barred.**

DOJ's own amicus in Magee acknowledged that the Court did not adopt a "physical-entry rule" and that websites/digital-services coverage remains open — but the 5th Cir. holding (operator's residence circuit) is adverse. **Realistic DOJ-side value is evidentiary, not enforcement.**

**Section 504:** Requires federal financial assistance or an executive agency. OpenAI's $200M DoD CDAO OTA (June 2025) does NOT extend §504 to consumer products — the §504 hook covers only the assisted program/activity, not general-use ChatGPT.

### 3.4 Section 508 / Accommodation-Request Route

The operator served a formal `GMRI_OpenAI_ADA_Notice` on **October 25, 2026**, requesting individualized written decision under 42 U.S.C. §§ 12102/12182, 28 C.F.R. §§ 36.301–36.303, and Texas HRC § 121.003. ### 3.5 FTC — Health Breach Notification Rule (16 CFR Part 318) + FTC Act §5

**GoodRx** ($1.5M civil penalty) and **Premom** ($1M) consent orders established that non-HIPAA "vendors of personal health records" and "PHR related entities" are covered. The 2024 amended rule (89 FR 45926, published May 30 2024) explicitly covers **"developers of many health applications."**
